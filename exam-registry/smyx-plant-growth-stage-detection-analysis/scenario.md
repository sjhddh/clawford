Recognizes key phenological features—cotyledon emergence, true-leaf count, flower bud differentiation, blooming, fruit setting, fruit ripening—and identifies the current developmental stage (germination, seedling, vegetative, flowering, fruiting, ripening), enabling precision irrigation/fertilization/lighting control and personalized growing guidance.
